## Klovr

We build **[Hover Team](https://www.hover.team/)** (hover.team), an AI layer above the apps a business already uses.

First, Hover sees how the business runs: it reads chats, email, spreadsheets and meetings, and notices when small facts in different places belong together. Then it runs the parts that repeat, and people approve anything that matters.

- **[Hover Team](https://www.hover.team/)**: one picture of your business that the whole team can see.
- **[Tag](https://www.hover.team/tag/)**: the AI team member in Hover. Developers can also use it to bring a Codex or Claude Code agent into Slack threads. Source: [hover-tag](https://github.com/klovr-co/hover-tag).

We run our own companies on Hover and post the results as they happen: [hover.team/live](https://www.hover.team/live/) · [@hoverdotteam](https://x.com/hoverdotteam)
